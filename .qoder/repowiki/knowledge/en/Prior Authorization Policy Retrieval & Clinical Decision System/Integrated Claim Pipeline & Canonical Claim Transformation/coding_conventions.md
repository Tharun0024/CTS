- Fail-closed error handling: every pipeline stage wraps its body in try/except and returns a `DecisionResponse` with `outcome=HUMAN_REVIEW` plus descriptive errors instead of raising.
- External subsystems are injected through a `components` dict keyed by role (e.g. `query_builder`, `exact_matcher`, `bge_embedder`, `faiss_retriever`, `bm25_retriever`, `candidate_pool`, `bge_reranker`, `policy_aggregator`, `deterministic_analyzer`, `evidence_builder`, `prompt_builder`, `llm_client`, `output_validator`), keeping the pipeline agnostic to concrete implementations.
- Claim versions are treated as immutable snapshots: new versions are created via `deepcopy(base_claim)` and appended to a `versions` list, with deltas recorded as `new_evidence_delta` IDs rather than mutating prior versions.
- Evidence selection follows a minimum-necessary principle: only records whose `evidence_key` was explicitly requested by Agent 1 or whose content matches a requested clinical concept are selected, and provenance against the original provider pool is validated before release.
- Sensitive evidence is filtered by a programmatic release gate that classifies each item as `ROUTINE` (released), `PROTECTED_*` (blocked), or `UNKNOWN` (blocked), with blocked items forcing escalation to `HUMAN_REVIEW`.
- Adaptation helpers use small private functions prefixed with underscore (e.g. `_coerce_to_list`, `_record_evidence_item`, `_extract_runtime_evidence`, `_classify_sensitivity`) to encapsulate shape normalization and classification logic.
